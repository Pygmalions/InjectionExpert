[Version 0.3.0]

In this version, we upgrade the emitting engine to EmitToolbox 0.3.0,
which provides better stability and helps us find bugs more easily.

Injection entries, such as 'InjectionConstantEntry' are now public classes;
corresponding API to add and remove entries are also provided.

'MemberInjector' has been reworked, and now developers can easily use 'InjectorOptions'
to have a finer grained control over the injection behavior, 
such as what members to inject,
and whether to record injected members and missing members.
This is useful for logging and debugging.

We also provide compatibility support for 'IServiceProvider' from 'Microsoft.Extensions.DependencyInjection':
from 'IInjectionProvider' to 'IServiceProvider' and from 'IServiceProvider' to 'IInjectionProvider'.
